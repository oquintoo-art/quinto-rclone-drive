Privacy Policy

This is a personal project for private file backups using rclone and Google Drive.

The project website does not collect, store, or process personal data through forms, analytics, or cookies.

File transfers are managed locally by the user through rclone and are subject to Google's privacy policies when Google Drive is used.

This website does not provide a public service or allow third-party users to connect their Google accounts.
